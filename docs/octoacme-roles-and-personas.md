# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Stakeholder Engagement Lead

### Role Summary
The Stakeholder Engagement Lead ensures clear communication and alignment across all project stakeholders. They manage stakeholder expectations, gather feedback, and facilitate engagement throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain stakeholder registry and engagement strategy
- Facilitate communication between project team and key stakeholders
- Escalate concerns and manage expectations
- Coordinate stakeholder feedback integration
- Conduct stakeholder reviews and feedback sessions
- Identify and mitigate stakeholder risks

### Goals
- Ensure stakeholder alignment and satisfaction
- Reduce communication gaps and misunderstandings
- Enable timely feedback integration into project decisions
- Maintain stakeholder engagement throughout delivery

### Interactions
- Works with Project Manager on communication planning and stakeholder coordination
- Reports stakeholder sentiment and concerns to Project Sponsor
- Collaborates with Product Manager on requirement validation and feedback
- Coordinates with Development Team on technical feasibility communications

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and progress updates
- Feedback collection and synthesis reports
- Stakeholder satisfaction surveys and reviews

---

## Quality Assurance Manager

### Role Summary
The Quality Assurance Manager defines quality standards and ensures rigorous testing throughout the project. They oversee test strategy, track quality metrics, and drive continuous improvement in product quality.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Oversee testing strategy and test execution
- Track defects and manage quality metrics
- Ensure compliance with quality benchmarks
- Collaborate on test planning and coverage
- Report quality status and trends

### Goals
- Deliver products that meet defined quality standards
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Improve overall product reliability and user satisfaction
- Establish data-driven quality metrics

### Interactions
- Collaborates with Development Team on test planning and design reviews
- Reports quality status to Project Manager and stakeholders
- Works with Release Coordinator to confirm release readiness
- Partners with Security and Compliance Officer on quality testing scope

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and quality reports
- Defect tracking and triage meetings
- Quality metrics dashboards and trend analysis

---

## Security and Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
The Security and Compliance Officer ensures the project meets security requirements and regulatory compliance standards. They guide security decision-making and oversee compliance throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and compliance standards
- Review architecture and design for security implications
- Conduct security testing and vulnerability assessment
- Maintain compliance documentation and audit trails
- Identify and escalate security and compliance risks
- Provide security guidance and training as needed

### Goals
- Protect project assets and customer data
- Maintain regulatory compliance and certifications
- Embed security into the development process
- Reduce security risks and vulnerabilities

### Interactions
- Advises Technical Architect on security design decisions and architecture
- Collaborates with Quality Assurance Manager on security testing scope
- Escalates risks to Project Manager and Project Sponsor
- Works with Development Team on secure coding practices
- Coordinates with Release Coordinator on compliance verification before release

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews and threat assessments
- Compliance checklists and audit documentation
- Security incident reports and risk registers

---

## Release Coordinator

### Role Summary
The Release Coordinator manages the end-to-end release process, from planning through deployment and monitoring. They ensure smooth transitions to production while maintaining quality and minimizing disruption.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and schedule release activities
- Coordinate deployment across environments
- Manage release communication and rollout
- Monitor post-release performance and issues
- Create and maintain release documentation
- Manage rollback procedures if needed

### Goals
- Execute predictable, low-risk releases
- Minimize downtime and disruption
- Ensure clear communication across all release participants
- Enable rapid response to post-release issues

### Interactions
- Works with Development Team to verify release readiness and artifact completeness
- Coordinates with Operations on deployment logistics and environment setup
- Reports release status and outcomes to Project Manager and stakeholders
- Collaborates with Quality Assurance Manager to confirm quality gates are met
- Partners with Security and Compliance Officer on compliance verification before go-live

### Typical Communication
- Release plans and deployment schedules
- Release notes and communication packages
- Deployment status updates and post-release reports

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Architect provides technical leadership and vision for the project. They make critical technology decisions, guide solution design, and ensure technical excellence throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical solution and architecture
- Make technology and framework decisions
- Provide technical guidance to development team
- Review code and design for technical excellence
- Identify and mitigate technical risks
- Ensure scalability, performance, and maintainability
- Mentor developers on technical best practices

### Goals
- Enable the delivery of technically sound solutions
- Reduce technical debt and complexity
- Optimize for scalability, performance, and maintainability
- Foster technical excellence and continuous learning

### Interactions
- Collaborates with Product Manager on requirements translation and technical feasibility
- Guides Development Team on implementation approach and technical decisions
- Works with Security and Compliance Officer on secure architecture design
- Advises Project Manager on technical risks and mitigation strategies
- Coordinates with Quality Assurance Manager on testability and performance requirements

### Typical Communication
- Architecture design documents and technical specifications
- Design reviews and technical guidance sessions
- Technical risk assessments and mitigation plans
